The last three rows are the ones the hackathon submission needs, and they are the reason the QNX SDP is the critical path (see ../verification-status.md).

Reproducing the Linux baseline

One command, from the repository root:

python3 tests/qemu/run_share_check.py --protos 9p --workload 30 \
        --trace baseline.log --workdir /tmp/passerelle-baseline

It boots a minimal Linux guest on the options qshare emits, runs 30 create/stat/read/unlink cycles on the share, and hands the QEMU trace to pbench roundtrips, which prints the table. Requirements: qemu-system-x86_64, virtiofsd (only for the virtiofs protocol), curl, cpio, gzip, unzstd, a readable host kernel with its modules. About 15 s per protocol.
